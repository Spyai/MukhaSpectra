# Risk register (ISO 14971 style)

> Research prototype — not for clinical use. This register is written now, while the software is
> small, because MukhaSpectra may become Software as a Medical Device. Each hazard lists the
> sequence of events, the harm, the controls built into the software, and how each control is
> verified. Severity (S) and probability (P) are qualitative, before controls: 1 (low) – 5 (high).

| ID | Hazard → sequence of events | Harm | S | P | Risk controls in software | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **False reassurance.** A lesion that needs biopsy is shown as LOW. Causes: dysplasia without FVL (thick keratin can stay bright), poor images, lesion outside captured sites, white patch dropped by segmentation as "teeth", diseased or dimmer reference site making relative AF look normal, look-alike discounts (plaque, pigment) applied to a real lesion, a red patch mistaken for pigment, encoded (gamma) pixel values treated as light, thresholds tuned on synthetic data. | Delayed referral, later-stage disease | 5 | 3 | Clinical red-flag **floors** the device can never lower (non-healing ulcer > 2 weeks, red patch, growth, neck lump, lump with a lesion, previous oral cancer with a lesion → HIGH; white patch, ulcer, trismus, bleeding → MODERATE). **Visual-lesion floor:** any lesion the health worker ticked or described (including "other") is at least MODERATE. **Symptom floors:** a reported lump, burning sensation, pain or previous oral cancer is at least MODERATE even without a lesion; every reported symptom is named in the explanation. **Strong-FVL floor:** discounts order findings but never take a region with ≥ 3.0 AF points before discounts, or mean relative AF ≤ 0.55, below MODERATE; when plaque or glare was discounted the worker is told to clean and retake. **Red-area floor:** WL a* ≥ 25 and L* ≤ 45 under an FVL region is "blood/red", never discounted and at least MODERATE; reddish pixels (a* ≥ 20) are never "pigmentation". Low-specificity and in-image reference discounts are not compounded at unpaired sites; lower gingiva is not down-weighted, so every site can reach MODERATE and every high-risk site HIGH on AF alone. **Linear-light analysis:** AF and dark frames are converted from sRGB to linear light before dark subtraction, flat field, exposure scaling and every AF ratio. **Reference max rule:** the paired site can only raise the reference (max of paired and own level); sites with a recorded lesion or appearance are never a reference; a dimmer opposite side adds `possible_bilateral_change`. **AF-based teeth refinement:** whitish areas that do not fluoresce like enamel go back into the mucosa; hole filling up to 35% of the frame keeps large white patches in; any remaining large excluded white area adds `white_area_not_analysed`. Porphyrin needs a real red excess, so deep FVL (high red/green) is not discounted as plaque. LOW requires every examined site analysed with a reliable mucosa outline (segmentation `ok`) and every lesion, including every lesion probe reading, checked; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. LOW wording: "does not rule out disease", routine re-screening, never "no referral". FVL on a reference site is still scored and flagged. Every result is "Pending clinician review". Thresholds are labelled baseline/unvalidated. | Fusion tests (floors, strong-FVL, visual-lesion, symptom and red-area floors, INCONCLUSIVE rule, segmentation-unreliable rule, habits never raise LOW, AF-only reachability per site), confounder colour tests, linear-light orchestrator tests (encoded files, unequal exposure), reference max-rule and bilateral tests, segmentation hole-fill and AF teeth-refinement tests, positioning tests |
| H2 | **False alarm / over-referral.** FVL caused by inflammation, plaque porphyrins, pigmentation, blood, glare or normal keratinised tissue. | Anxiety, wasted referral capacity, loss of trust in screening | 3 | 4 | Confounder detection (red porphyrin excess, pigmentation, blood, small specular glare) discounts AF points and is shown per region (bounded by the H1 floors). Diascopy result (FVL resolves under pressure) discounts AF when the site has exactly one dark region. Lower AF weight at upper gingiva, tongue dorsum, hard palate. Possible bilateral change and excluded white areas are reported as limitations rather than raising the level. MODERATE path is "remove irritants, re-examine in 2 weeks" before referral. Clinician review. | AF confounder tests, fusion discount tests |
| H3 | **Output read as a diagnosis** or as an all-clear. Wording implies the device diagnoses or that the mouth is fine. | Patient skips biopsy, or undue alarm | 4 | 3 | Positioning guard bans diagnosis / malignant / cancer detected / no cancer / benign / tumour / carcinoma / all clear outside mandated disclaimers, across UI strings, report HTML/PDF, API output and configs. Generated text never calls the comparison tissue "healthy", never says "no referral", and the placeholder spectral label reads "no spectral difference detected (does not rule out disease)". Banner "Research prototype — not for clinical use" on every screen and report page. Disclaimer box on every report. | `test_positioning`, report and frontend positioning scans, demo-result wording test, report "healthy" scan, console strings test |
| H4 | **Reliance on an unvalidated model.** A placeholder or untrained model drives the risk level. | Wrong triage | 4 | 3 | WL classifier ships `model_unavailable`; when unavailable it contributes nothing. All models carry `validated: false` (schema enforces it). Spectral classifier returns status `placeholder` (never "ok"); the report prints "Heuristic placeholder (not a trained model, not validated)" and "No spectral difference detected (does not rule out disease)" instead of "not suspicious". Its comparison uses the probe-independent lesion/reference quotient. No accuracy numbers anywhere. Model status shown in report and UI. | WL classifier tests, fusion "WL unavailable contributes nothing" test, schema test `validated` default |
| H5 | **Synthetic data mistaken for real**, or used to support a performance claim. | Misleading evidence, regulatory harm | 4 | 2 | SYNTHETIC stamped in image pixels, file names, metadata (`synthetic: true`, provenance), result notice, UI ribbon and report watermark on every page. `ml/` rules forbid synthetic images in evaluation sets. | Synthetic watermark tests, report watermark test, UI ribbon test |
| H6 | **Left/right or site mix-up.** | Biopsy at wrong site, missed lesion | 4 | 2 | Site recorded in every capture's metadata. Site map drawn facing the patient with an explicit orientation note. Contralateral pairing defined in one catalogue. | Site catalogue tests, site map rendering tests |
| H7 | **WL↔AF misregistration.** Heatmap overlays the wrong tissue. | Wrong biopsy target | 3 | 3 | Registration confidence with sanity limits on translation/scale; low confidence fails QC and blocks analysis of that site; retake prompt. | Registration tests (recovery, failure → identity + not passed), QC registration tests |
| H8 | **Calibration drift** between devices or days. | Wrong absolute intensities | 3 | 3 | Daily calibration with expiry; missing or expired calibration marks results "uncalibrated" in the report. Decisions rely on within-patient relative AF (contralateral), which cancels device gain. AF is flat-fielded with the violet-LED profile from the fluorescence card (never the white-light profile); a calibration without it adds `af_flat_field_missing`. Spectral comparison uses the lesion/reference quotient, which cancels the probe's per-channel gain. | Calibration status and AF flat-field tests, spectral device-invariance tests |
| H9 | **Ambient light contamination** of AF or spectral readings. | Masked FVL, wrong spectra | 3 | 3 | Dark frame QC; warning when no dark frame. Spectral ambient-leak and contact checks. | QC dark frame and spectral QC tests |
| H10 | **PII exposure** (including through an exposed server or uploaded file metadata). | Privacy harm (DPDP Act 2023) | 4 | 2 | PII in a separate table keyed by pseudonymous ID; results and the screening detail API carry no name. Consent with a recorded method (checkbox, signature, thumbprint) required before anything is stored. **API key:** optional shared `MUKHA_API_KEY` (≥ 16 characters) required on every `/api/v1` route except `/health`; without it the server logs a warning and `/version` reports `authentication: none`; compose binds to 127.0.0.1 and checks the Host header. The console's nginx adds the key server-side, so the console port must stay local or behind network access control. EXIF/XMP/IPTC/comments (GPS, camera serials) are stripped from stored JPEG/PNG uploads (HEIC/DNG/TIFF/WebP/BMP still stored as uploaded). **Erase endpoints:** `DELETE /screenings/{id}` and `DELETE /patients/{id}` remove rows, identity, inputs, results and cached PDFs (which contain the name) and write PII-free audit events. (Phase 5: per-user authentication, RBAC, encryption at rest.) | API tests: no PII in result JSON or detail endpoint; consent-missing stores nothing; API-key and host tests; metadata stripping tests; erasure tests (nothing left in DB or storage) |
| H11 | **Silent overwrite of a result.** | Clinician acts on a changed report without knowing | 3 | 2 | Re-analysis writes a new result version with `supersedes_result_id`; reports are versioned and stored. | API reanalysis/version tests |
| H12 | **Report mixed up between patients.** | Wrong patient referred | 4 | 2 | Report ID, pseudonymous ID and QR link to the record on every report. | Report tests |
| H13 | **Delayed action on HIGH.** | Progression | 5 | 2 | HIGH action is "urgent referral" with a timeline in days and steps. (Phase 4: immediate notification of HIGH to the review queue.) | Fusion action tests |
| H14 | **Violet LED exposure** (Phase 3 hardware). | Eye or tissue discomfort | 3 | 2 | Firmware auto-off ≤ 10 s per exposure, hard duty-cycle cap. | Firmware tests (Phase 3) |
| H15 | **Spectral probe without contact** or saturated. | Wrong spectral features | 2 | 3 | Contact (CLEAR/NIR), saturation, repeatability and signal checks; failed QC skips the spectral classifier. | Spectral QC tests |
| H16 | **Untraceable result** (unknown thresholds or software version). | Cannot investigate an incident | 3 | 2 | Every result records software (`<version>+<build id>`, from `MUKHA_BUILD_ID` or git), pipeline, schema, model and config versions with SHA-256 of each config file; the report prints them. | Schema/version tests, report header test, build-id tests |
| H17 | **Service exhaustion** at a camp: decompression bombs, oversized or chunked uploads, deeply nested JSON, too many concurrent analyses. | Screening stops for everyone; data loss | 3 | 3 | Images over 50 MP refused; uploads shrunk while decoding (≤ 2048 px) under a per-request decoded-pixel budget (180 MP, enough for every site with WL + AF + dark); byte limits enforced while streaming (chunked bodies included); manifest ≤ 1 MB in any form; nesting depth limited (422); non-finite numbers rejected; at most `max_concurrent_analyses` (2) analyses, others 503 with Retry-After. | Ingest review-fix tests (budget 413 before storing, streaming limits, nesting), concurrency tests |
| H18 | **Software error shown as a capture problem** (or hidden). | Worker retakes endlessly, or a site is silently skipped | 3 | 2 | A crash in either analysis pass makes that site `not_analysed` with a software-error note and the `analysis_error` limitation; it is never listed under failed QC or sent for a retake; LOW is impossible; the report says "Not analysed (software error) — examine clinically". | Orchestrator crash tests, fusion and report integration tests |

Residual risks accepted for the research prototype: thresholds are not clinically validated
(H1, H2); bilateral disease can mask relative FVL (H1, mitigated by the reference max rule, the
`possible_bilateral_change` limitation, clinical floors and review); a mild, small FVL region
(relative AF 0.55–0.75 in linear light) with no other finding can still be LOW (H1); a brightly
fluorescing hyperkeratotic white patch can stay excluded as "teeth" (H1, reported as
`white_area_not_analysed`); no learned model yet (H4); no per-user authentication until Phase 5
(H10). None of these is acceptable for clinical use; clinical validation with
biopsy-linked ground truth (Phase 4 flywheel) is required before any change in positioning.
